# YouCam submission checklist

Preparation only. No registration, repository creation, publication, deployment or final submission is established by this checklist.

**Deadline:** 3 November 2026, 00:45 Asia/Taipei. **Free judge access:** maintain through 21 November 2026, 00:45 Asia/Taipei, based on the official judging period reviewed for the brief. Confirm the live event page again before final submission.

## Must work before submission

- [ ] Register through the authorized account and preserve a genuine confirmation.
- [ ] Complete YouCam email verification; obtain and redeem the actual activity code. Do not assume the 1,000 units have arrived.
- [ ] Verify the account-compatible host, available units and feature cost with read-only checks.
- [ ] Complete one genuine `cloth-v3` upper-body task using cleared materials; then complete the actual two-look workflow.
- [ ] Verify consent, failure states, duplicate prevention, result ownership, Clear and budget protection on the release candidate.
- [ ] Verify desktop/mobile behavior and English, Traditional Chinese and Japanese text/glyphs.
- [ ] Complete [asset provenance](ASSETS.md) and privacy/retention disclosures, including an operator contact and cleanup schedule before public personal-photo use.

## Required materials

- [ ] Real working application: at least one YouCam API with clear consumer or eCommerce value.
- [ ] Functional demo URL or test build with complete English instructions and necessary private test access. Keep it free and usable through judging.
- [ ] Complete code/assets/setup instructions in a reviewable repository. Public code needs appropriate licensing; if private, share only as the official rules specify.
- [ ] English feature/value description; otherwise provide complete English translations.
- [ ] Actual 1–3 minute public YouTube (preferred) or Vimeo demo, showing the API and end-to-end application behavior.
- [ ] Truthful original-work or significant-update description. Never treat direct old-project resubmission as approved merely because no broad cross-submission ban was found.
- [ ] Final link, source/asset and secret scan: no keys, signed URLs, private paths, raw provider responses or personal-photo files in public material.
- [ ] Submit the entry, reopen the final page and preserve the real confirmation URL/time. Saving a portfolio draft is not final submission.

## Entrant information: use facts, not guesses

Already confirmed by the user: individual participation, Taiwan residence and adult status. Reuse these where applicable; they do not establish every sponsor-specific declaration.

- [ ] Confirm the actual form's name/display-name/legal-name and contact fields from the account or the entrant. Do not invent a legal name, address or telephone number.
- [ ] Review any event-specific employment, sponsor/judge relationship and conflict declarations.
- [ ] Confirm any required first-person learning, satisfaction or intent-to-reuse answers with the entrant; do not fabricate feelings.
- [ ] Do not select a student or women-in-tech award without confirmed eligibility. General prizes do not require these identities.
- [ ] Confirm permission to publish the person imagery and final video separately from consent to process it.
- [ ] If selected as a winner, arrange the mandatory exit interview and any official winner-verification process then; those are not completed now.
