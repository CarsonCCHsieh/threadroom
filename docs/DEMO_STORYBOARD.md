## Capture checklist

- Record a verified release, readable cursor actions and an English interface/narration or complete English subtitles.
- Show the actual API used and a real end-to-end outcome on the target device. Do not spend the video on a slide deck.
- Keep the final length between one and three minutes. The official rules do not require judges to watch beyond three minutes.
- Any accelerated generation must be a truthful edit of that same task. No fake percentage progress, fabricated timings or hidden substitution.
- Do not show API keys, console account details, signed URLs, full private ledgers, desktop user paths or private notifications.
- Use cleared sample assets or separately consented public footage. Consent to processing is not automatically permission to publish a person's appearance.
- No unlicensed music, retailer logos or copyrighted footage. Plain narration and app audio are sufficient.
- If a feature fails verification, omit its demonstration and correct the narration/draft. A planned shot is not permission to claim success.
- Publish only when authorized through the coordinating agent. Save the real video URL and upload confirmation before marking this step complete.
